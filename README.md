## 1. What is CRM?

CRM (Customer Relationship Management) is a system used by businesses to manage customer interactions, sales, support, and marketing activities in one centralized platform.

### Benefits of CRM
- Improves customer relationships
- Centralizes customer data
- Increases sales efficiency
- Automates business processes
- Enhances customer support

## 2. Why Companies Use Salesforce?

Salesforce is a cloud-based CRM platform used worldwide because it provides:

- Scalability
- Cloud accessibility
- Automation tools
- Real-time analytics
- App integrations
- Secure customer data management

## 3. Important Salesforce Objects

### Account
Represents a company, organization, or customer.

### Contact
Represents an individual associated with an Account.

### Opportunity
Represents a potential sales deal or revenue opportunity.

## 5. Questions & Answers

### Q1: What is the primary goal of using CRM?

**Answer:**  
The main goal of CRM is to improve customer relationships, streamline business operations, and increase profitability.

### Q2: How are Accounts and Contacts related?

**Answer:**  
One Account can contain multiple Contacts associated with that organization.

### Q3: When should an Opportunity be created?

**Answer:**  
An Opportunity should be created when there is a qualified potential sale that can be tracked through different sales stages.
